Job Title: Senior Machine Learning Engineer

Must have

- Hands-on experience deploying and monitoring models in production on a public cloud
- Experience with LLMs / GenAI (RAG, prompt engineering, or agents)

Nice to have
- Spark / large-scale data processing
- Experience mentoring or leading a small team
- Experience with LangChain / LangGraph or similar frameworks
