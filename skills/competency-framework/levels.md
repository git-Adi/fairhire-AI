# Level Guide

| Level | Title | Scope | Evidence of this level |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Learner | Completes well-defined tasks with guidance | Coursework, tutorials, small personal projects |
| L2 | Practitioner | Owns features end to end within a team | Shipped features to production, writes tests, reviews peers' code |
| L3 | Expert | Owns systems; makes design decisions for the team | Designed a system used in production, measurable business impact, mentors others |
| L4 | Leader | Sets technical direction across teams | Defined standards or platforms adopted by multiple teams, org-level impact |

## Mapping years to levels (rough guide only)
- 0-2 years usually L1-L2
- 3-6 years usually L2-L3
- 7+ years usually L3-L4

Always prefer the evidence column over years.
